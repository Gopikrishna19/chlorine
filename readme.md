# Pool Chlorine Calculator

Calculate the required amount of chlorine to maintain your swimming pool.

Visit [Pool Chlorine Calculator](https://gopikrishna19.github.io/chlorine) to use the calculator.

> [!important]
> This tool is for quick household pool estimates only, scaled from a 30 ppm CYA baseline. It is
> provided as-is with no liability; use at your own risk. Calculations assume ideal water conditions and
> exclude SLAM-specific balancing. If you are SLAMming, focus on hardness and total alkalinity and ignore pH
> for this quick estimate.
